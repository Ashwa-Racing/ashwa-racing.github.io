Ashwa Racing is the Formula Student team of RV College of Engineering in Bengaluru. Students across engineering and business disciplines work together to design, manufacture, test, and present a formula-style race car.

Community contributions help close the gap between a design on screen and a car we can test. Supporters can opt in to have their name displayed on the car, and qualifying contributions include an Ashwa Racing competition shirt shipped to them.
